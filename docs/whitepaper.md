# ELYQUID Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** A trading culture on Elysium where activity has direction, and where information is open to everyone at the same moment.

**Mission.** Give the community a calm, honest view of the market and a shared home for the token, starting with a board that shows what is moving and how deep the water is beneath it.

## Roadmap

1. **Foundation.** Brand, site and live market board. The contract address is announced on X first and then appears at the top of the site.
2. **Community.** Holder circles, ambassador roles, quests tied to on-chain activity, and open progress notes.
3. **Reach.** Integrations with Elysium venues and tools, richer analytics on the board, and partnerships across the network.
